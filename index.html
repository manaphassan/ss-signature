💡 How to Paste in Gmail / Outlook:
  1. Click "Copy Signature for Email" above.
  2. Open your email client settings (e.g. Gmail Settings → General → Signature).

  4. Click Save Changes at the bottom of the page.

✨ Best Practice Checklist

  • Always use standard paste (Ctrl+V): Do not use "Paste as plain text" (Ctrl+Shift+V), otherwise the formatting, layout, and colors will be stripped.
  • Dark Mode Verification: Use the 🌙 Dark Client toggle above to inspect how recipient devices running dark mode will display your signature.
  • Booking CTA Button: For doctors, consultants, and patient care managers, keep the "Clinic Consultation Booking CTA" enabled to drive direct patient bookings.

❓ Troubleshooting Common Issues

  • Signature appears small or broken in Outlook: Outlook Desktop uses Microsoft Word's HTML engine. The "Copy Signature for Email" rich-text clipboard command formats tables specifically for Microsoft Office paste handlers.
  • Company Logo doesn't appear: Ensure your email client does not block external images by default in preview.
