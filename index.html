📧 How to Use Your Email Signature in Gmail

Step 1: Generate Your Signature

  1. Fill in your information in the form above
  2. Select your office location
  3. Click "Generate" button

Step 2: Copy the Signature

Choose one of these methods:

  • Method A: Click 📋 Copy button (automatic)
  • Method B: Click 👆 Select All for Manual Copy, then press Ctrl+C

Step 3: Paste into Gmail

  1. Go to Gmail Settings
  2. Scroll down to the "Signature" section
  3. Click "Create new" or select existing signature
  4. Click in the signature editor box
  5. Press Ctrl+V (Windows) or Cmd+V (Mac)
  6. Your formatted signature appears with logo and colors!
  7. Click "Save Changes" at the bottom

💡 Important Tips

  • Use regular paste (Ctrl+V), NOT "Paste as plain text"
  • The signature includes your office-specific logo
  • All SS Health brand colors are preserved
  • Make sure images (logo, banner) are accessible

🔧 Troubleshooting

If signature pastes as plain text:

  • Try Method B (Select All + Ctrl+C)
  • Make sure you're using a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)
  • Paste directly in Gmail signature editor, not compose window
